The Comparison

Atlas vs Jan, honestly

Every fact sourced from their own pages. Checked November 4, 2026.

Jan is the respected open-source answer to ChatGPT: a local chat app built in public by Menlo Research, free under the Apache 2.0 license, with millions of downloads. Atlas respects it — and is not trying to be it. Where Jan is a chat app you can rebuild as your own, Atlas is one assistant, finished and personal: it remembers you in files you own, cites its sources, and sizes itself to your machine. Every fact about Jan below comes from their own public pages, checked November 4, 2026. Where their pages don’t advertise a feature, we say exactly that — not that it doesn’t exist.

Where they’re genuinely good — we mean this

Jan is what open source is supposed to look like: Apache 2.0, built in public, millions of downloads earned one honest release at a time, no account needed to start. If your first question about any tool is “can I read the code, fork it, and make it mine?” — Jan is your answer, and it’s a good one. We won’t pretend otherwise to win a row in a table.

Our opinion — labelled as opinion, because it is

In our opinion, the difference is what you’re handed. Jan hands you an excellent, open workshop — bring models, bring keys if you like, make it yours. Atlas hands you a person: memory already on and written in files you can hold, answers that show their sources or honestly refuse, a brain already sized to your machine. Their memory is, by their own homepage, still “Coming Soon.” Ours is the product. If you want to tinker, take Jan with our blessing. If you want to be remembered, take Atlas.
