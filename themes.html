One law holds in every theme: the orb's colours are a language, not a decoration. Teal is home. Brighter means speaking. Amber means a problem — only a problem, in every theme. The ice-blue rim means the web is on. A theme dresses the room; it never rewrites the language. And a theme is data, not code — colours, wallpaper, sounds. Nothing in a theme can touch your files, your memory, or how Atlas thinks.
