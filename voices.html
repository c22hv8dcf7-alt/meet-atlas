Honest terms

What picking means — today

Picking a voice here is an audition, nothing more and nothing less. Your pick is remembered on this device, in your browser only — no account, no form, and nothing about your choice ever leaves this machine, which is rather the point of Atlas. When your Atlas wakes up with the Voice chapter, this roster is who he can speak as — and the voice you picked here is the one he'll greet you in first.

A voice is data, not code: a small sound file an engine reads aloud with. Swap it or delete it any time, and deleting one just brings the default back. And one law holds in every voice, the way it holds in every theme: the meaning never changes. Warm or deadpan, British or American — Atlas still shows his sources, still says "I don't know" out loud, and still belongs to you.
