Free software, kept free on purpose

Atlas has no subscription because it has no server to pay for — it lives on your Mac. What it does have is one developer, a test Mac, and time. If Atlas saves you the price of a subscription — or a $114.99 stick — and you’d like to help it stay free for the next person, you can chip in here.

Support is voluntary and changes nothing about what you get: the free Atlas is the whole Atlas. There is no paid tier, and there never will be a version where the free one is crippled to upsell you.

Support Atlas on Ko-fi

Plain words about money: Porch Light is not a registered charity, so support is not tax-deductible, and it counts as income for the publisher. It is a gift toward keeping free software free — never a purchase, and refunds of gifts aren’t a thing we can offer. Thank you for even reading this far down the page.
